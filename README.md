## Projects

### [roadway.report](https://roadway.report) — Nationwide Vision Zero Map - [`repo`](https://github.com/bencarneiro/ntsb)

An interactive map of every recorded traffic fatality in the US (2001–2024), built on federal crash data.

- Click any street and see every fatality recorded there, with filters across hundreds of variables the feds track
- The backend API isn't tied to one region — other devs can build their own frontend for any part of the US using it
- Takes a big, messy government dataset and makes it fast to search from the browser

### [censusmaps.org](https://censusmaps.org) — Interactive Demographic Maps - [`repo`](https://github.com/bencarneiro/maps)

Free 3D demographic maps for any US metro — no GIS background needed.

- Interactive maps you can make and share with just a URL
- Export the GeoJSON layers to use in your own tools
- Used by urban planners and demographers as a quick reference

### [transit.observer](https://transit.observer) — US Transit Spending & Performance - [`repo`](https://github.com/bencarneiro/transportation-bots)

Explore transit spending and performance for any agency in the US, going back to 1985.

- See capital vs. operating spend by agency and year
- Compare service levels, ridership, and cost-efficiency across agencies over time
